# Investor Film — Voice-over draft

Target length: ~78 seconds.

## 0:00–0:06 — The problem

> The first generation of AI won on answers. But people rarely change because of a single answer.

## 0:06–0:14 — Mentor And I

> Mentor And I is building something different: a mentoring relationship that remembers the person, the goals and the context over time.

## 0:14–0:26 — Eight specialist mentors

> One relationship connects eight specialist mentors — from life decisions and ADHD to relationships, stress, parenting, health, focus and charisma.

## 0:26–0:42 — Product demonstration

> A user can start broadly with Marcus. When a specialist becomes relevant, the next mentor can work from the context that already matters instead of starting again from zero.

## 0:42–0:56 — Compounding context

> Goals, recurring patterns, preferences and progress can accumulate across conversations. The product is not only the next answer. It is the growing understanding behind it.

## 0:56–1:08 — Investor thesis

> The model starts with a free entry point, expands into specialist access, subscriptions and credits, and uses evidence-led mentor content as an additional acquisition surface.

## 1:08–1:18 — Category

> As foundation models improve, we believe differentiation moves toward context, specialization, trust and continuity. Mentor And I is building for that category: persistent AI mentorship.

## End card

> Mentor And I. The mentor who remembers you.
