The marketing website does not use advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies, and does not currently rely on non-essential analytics.

This Cookie Policy explains how Mentor And I uses cookies and similar browser technologies.

Marketing website

The current Mentor And I marketing website does not intentionally use advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies. It also does not currently rely on non-essential analytics cookies.

The website may load technical resources required to display the site, such as fonts and other static assets.

Mentor And I application

When a user enters the Mentor And I application, strictly necessary browser technologies may be used for purposes such as:

  • authentication;
  • maintaining a secure session;
  • security;
  • remembering essential service state.

These technologies are required for requested account functionality.

Future analytics or marketing tools

If Mentor And I later introduces analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies that require consent under applicable law, those technologies must not be activated until the user has been given the required choice.

Where consent is required, users must be able to accept or reject non-essential cookies and later change their choice.

Browser controls

Browsers generally allow users to inspect, block or delete cookies.

Blocking cookies that are strictly necessary for authentication may prevent parts of the Mentor And I application from functioning correctly.

Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy if the technologies used by the website or application change.
