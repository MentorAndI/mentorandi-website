Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Mentoring conversations are personal, so this policy sets out plainly what we process, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Mentor And I is designed around ongoing mentoring relationships, which means privacy is particularly important.

This Privacy Policy explains how Mentor AI Corp, trading as Mentor And I (“we”, “us”, “our”), collects and processes personal data when you use our website, application and mentoring services.

1. Data controller

The data controller is:

Mentor AI Corp
1309 Coffeen Avenue STE 1200
Sheridan, Wyoming 82801
United States
Email: support@mentorandi.com

2. Information we may process

Depending on how you use Mentor And I, we may process:

Account information

Such as your email address, account identifiers, authentication information and account status.

Profile and onboarding information

Such as goals, preferences, areas you want help with and information you choose to provide during onboarding.

Mentoring conversations

Messages you send to mentors and AI-generated responses.

Memory, goals and reflections

Information derived from your interactions that helps the service maintain continuity between conversations.

Feedback

Ratings, feedback messages, bug reports and related page or mentor context.

Messages you send us

If you write to us through the contact form or by email, we process the name, email address, topic and message you provide, together with the time of sending. We use this only to answer you and to keep a record of the exchange. The contact form does not set cookies and does not use any third-party service.

Subscription and billing information

Information about your plan, subscription status and transactions where paid services are enabled. Payment-card information may be processed directly by the payment provider rather than stored by Mentor And I.

Technical and security information

Such as IP address, device and browser information, authentication events, server logs and information needed to prevent abuse and maintain security.

Usage information

Such as request counts, mentor used, provider and model metadata, approximate processing cost and technical usage information. We do not need to store the full content of a conversation merely to calculate operational usage statistics.

3. Sensitive information

Mentoring conversations can sometimes contain information that is sensitive. You control what you choose to tell the service.

Mentor And I is not a medical-record system and users should not upload medical records or other sensitive documents unless a feature specifically requests them.

Where information you voluntarily provide falls within a legally protected special category of personal data, we will process it only where an appropriate legal basis under applicable data-protection law exists.

Certain information processed by Mentor And I may constitute consumer health data under United States state privacy laws, including information concerning mental or behavioural health and information inferred from your conversations. Additional disclosures and rights are described in our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

4. Why we process personal data

We process personal data to:

  • create and secure your account;
  • provide Mentor And I and generate mentor responses;
  • maintain conversation history;
  • provide memory and continuity;
  • personalise the mentoring experience;
  • maintain goals and reflections;
  • apply plan and usage limits;
  • provide customer support;
  • receive and review feedback;
  • monitor reliability, abuse and security;
  • understand aggregate product performance;
  • meet legal and regulatory obligations;
  • improve the service in ways consistent with this Privacy Policy.

Personal user memories are not automatically converted into shared knowledge for other users.

5. Legal bases

Depending on the processing activity, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases:

Performance of a contract — where processing is necessary to provide the service you requested.

Legitimate interests — for proportionate activities such as service security, abuse prevention, reliability and product administration.

Consent — where applicable law requires consent for a particular activity.

Legal obligation — where processing is necessary to comply with applicable law.

Where consent is the legal basis, you may withdraw that consent, subject to applicable law.

6. AI processing

Mentor And I uses artificial-intelligence model providers to generate mentor responses. Information necessary to generate a response may therefore be transmitted to contracted AI service providers acting under applicable contractual and privacy arrangements.

We do not sell private mentoring conversations to advertisers.

We do not provide employers with access to individual private mentoring conversations merely because an employer pays for access to a service.

7. Service providers

We may use service providers for:

  • hosting and infrastructure;
  • authentication;
  • databases and storage;
  • AI model processing;
  • email delivery;
  • payment processing;
  • security and monitoring;
  • customer support.

These providers receive only the information reasonably necessary for the relevant service and are subject to appropriate contractual obligations where required.

8. International data transfers

Some technology providers may process information outside the country in which you live.

Where personal data is transferred internationally, we use legally recognised transfer mechanisms and safeguards where required by applicable law.

9. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including operation of your account, continuity of mentoring, security and legal obligations.

Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

Account and mentoring data may be deleted or anonymised following account deletion, subject to limited information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention or accounting purposes.

10. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • access your personal data;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • request deletion;
  • restrict certain processing;
  • object to certain processing;
  • receive certain data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection authority.

The application may also provide direct data-export and deletion controls.

Requests can be sent to support@mentorandi.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

11. Account deletion

Users may request deletion of their Mentor And I data.

Deletion may not be instantaneous across backups and technical systems, but deleted data will no longer be used as active mentoring context once the relevant deletion process has completed.

Certain minimal records may be retained where legally required or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention or dispute resolution.

12. Security

We use technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect personal information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

13. Children

The current Mentor And I service is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not intentionally offer the current service directly to children.

14. Marketing

Where we send optional marketing communications, users will be provided with a way to unsubscribe.

Service messages required for account operation or security are not marketing communications.

15. Automated processing

Mentor And I uses automated systems to generate responses and organise context.

The service is designed as an AI mentoring tool rather than a system that independently makes legally binding or similarly significant decisions about users.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service changes or legal requirements develop. The current version and date will always be published on this page.
