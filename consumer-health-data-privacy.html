Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Conversations about stress, focus, sleep or how you are feeling can amount to health data under some United States state laws, even though Mentor And I is not a healthcare service. This policy sets out what that means in practice.

Some information that you voluntarily discuss with Mentor And I may qualify as consumer health data under certain United States state laws. This policy supplements our general Privacy Policy; where the two differ on consumer health data, this policy applies.

1. Consumer health data we may process

Depending on what you choose to discuss, consumer health data may include information concerning:

  • mental or emotional wellbeing;
  • stress or burnout;
  • psychological or behavioural experiences;
  • sleep, exercise or general wellness;
  • executive-function or attention-related challenges;
  • health conditions or symptoms you voluntarily disclose;
  • medication information you voluntarily disclose;
  • social, psychological or behavioural interventions you discuss; and
  • memories, goals, reflections, classifications or other information derived or inferred from what you tell us, where that information may reveal health status.

Mentor And I does not require you to provide medical records, and you are never obliged to discuss your health with a mentor.

2. Where it comes from

We may receive consumer health data:

  • directly from you;
  • through mentoring conversations;
  • through onboarding responses;
  • through goals or feedback you create; and
  • through product-generated memories, reflections or inferences derived from what you have told us.

3. Why we process it

We process consumer health data only for disclosed purposes:

  • providing the mentoring service you have asked for;
  • maintaining relevant conversation continuity and memory;
  • personalising mentoring responses;
  • operating safety safeguards;
  • securing and maintaining the service;
  • responding to your requests; and
  • complying with legal obligations.

4. Sharing

Mentor And I may provide information to service providers acting on our behalf where reasonably necessary to operate the service. The categories are the same as those set out in our Privacy Policy: hosting and infrastructure; authentication; databases and storage; AI model processing; email delivery; payment processing; security and monitoring; and customer support.

These providers receive only the information reasonably necessary for the relevant service and are subject to appropriate contractual obligations where required.

Mentor And I does not sell consumer health data.

Mentor And I does not use private mentoring conversations or consumer health data for targeted advertising.

5. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have the right to:

  • confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data;
  • access your consumer health data;
  • obtain information about certain recipients;
  • withdraw consent to future collection or sharing where consent applies;
  • request deletion; and
  • appeal a decision we make about a privacy request.

Requests can be sent to support@mentorandi.com. We may take reasonable steps to authenticate a request before disclosing or deleting personal information.

6. How long we keep it

Consumer health data is kept under the same retention approach as the rest of your personal information, described in section 9 of our Privacy Policy. In short: only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected for, with account and mentoring data deleted or anonymised following account deletion, subject to limited information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention or accounting purposes.

7. Changes

If we materially expand the categories of consumer health data we collect, the purposes it is used for, or the entities it is shared with, we will make the required disclosures and obtain consent where applicable law requires it.

The current version and date are always published on this page.
