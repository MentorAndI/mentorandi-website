AI Safety

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Mentor And I is built to provide AI mentoring, not human companionship, therapy or emergency care. This page explains the automated safeguards in place when a conversation involves suicide, self-harm or immediate danger — and, just as importantly, their limits.

Mentor And I is not a crisis or emergency service. If you believe that you or another person is in immediate danger, may attempt suicide or self-harm, or needs urgent medical or psychiatric help, contact your local emergency service or a qualified crisis service now.

1. AI, not human

All Mentor And I mentors are artificial intelligence. Mentor personas have names, portraits and conversational styles, but they are not human beings and do not claim to be human.

An AI mentor is not a licensed therapist, psychologist, physician or other healthcare professional, and a conversation with one does not create a professional relationship. Mentor And I provides mentoring, coaching, educational and self-help support.

2. What is in place today

We would rather describe less than we have than more, so this section covers only safeguards that are implemented and tested today.

Before an ordinary mentor response is generated, Mentor And I runs a separate automated safety check for language indicating self-harm, suicide or immediate danger. The safety layer classifies the message independently of the mentor persona.

When the system classifies a message as high risk, it interrupts the normal mentoring flow and returns a deterministic safety response that directs the user toward immediate human or emergency support. The high-risk message is not sent to the ordinary mentor language-model response path and the safety response does not consume mentor credits.

The safety layer is designed to fail closed: a failure in the safety classifier does not simply allow the normal mentor response to proceed. Safety regression tests are part of the application launch checks.

3. Limits of the safety layer

Automated safety systems are imperfect. The classifier can miss genuine danger, can react to language that was not intended as a crisis statement, and cannot determine a person's actual clinical risk or circumstances.

The safety layer is not human monitoring, diagnosis, treatment or emergency response. Mentor And I does not guarantee that every crisis will be detected or handled correctly.

4. What this means for you

Do not rely on Mentor And I as your only way to identify or respond to an emergency. If you believe that you or another person may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or qualified human support directly.

Do not delay contacting a doctor, crisis service or emergency service because of anything a mentor has said.

Mentor And I is not continuously monitored by a human professional, and messages sent through it are not read in real time.

5. Limits of automated safety systems

Even where automated safety systems exist, they have real limits. They cannot identify every crisis, cannot determine a person's actual level of risk, and can be wrong in both directions — missing genuine danger and reacting to language that was never dangerous.

No safety system we build will change the advice above: when immediate help is needed, contact a human crisis or emergency service.

6. Continuous improvement

Safety rules, model safeguards and evaluation procedures may be updated as the service develops, as applicable law changes and as better safety methods become available.

This page is maintained to match what the product actually does. If you find a discrepancy between what is described here and how Mentor And I behaves, please tell us at support@mentorandi.com.
