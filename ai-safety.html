AI Safety

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Mentor And I is built to provide AI mentoring, not human companionship, therapy or emergency care. This page explains what safeguards are in place when a conversation involves suicide, self-harm or immediate danger — and, just as importantly, what is not in place yet.

Mentor And I is not a crisis or emergency service. If you believe that you or another person is in immediate danger, may attempt suicide or self-harm, or needs urgent medical or psychiatric help, contact your local emergency service or a qualified crisis service now.

1. AI, not human

All Mentor And I mentors are artificial intelligence. Mentor personas have names, portraits and conversational styles, but they are not human beings and do not claim to be human.

An AI mentor is not a licensed therapist, psychologist, physician or other healthcare professional, and a conversation with one does not create a professional relationship. Mentor And I provides mentoring, coaching, educational and self-help support.

2. What is in place today

We would rather describe less than we have than more, so this section covers only what the service actually does at the time of writing.

Mentor And I gives every mentor a standing instruction not to present itself as a substitute for medical or emergency professionals. Where a conversation shows signs of immediate danger or self-harm, that instruction directs the mentor to encourage the person to contact local emergency services or qualified human support.

This is guidance given to a language model. It shapes how a mentor is likely to respond; it does not guarantee how a mentor will respond in every case.

3. What is not in place yet

Mentor And I does not currently have:

  • an automated system that detects language indicating suicidal ideation or self-harm;
  • risk classification of conversations;
  • a deterministic override that interrupts the normal mentoring flow when a safety concern appears; or
  • a dedicated crisis-handling path separate from ordinary mentoring.

These safeguards are under development. We will describe them here once they are implemented and tested, and not before. We would rather this page be uncomfortably plain than reassuring and wrong.

4. What this means for you

Do not rely on Mentor And I to notice that you are in danger. It may not.

Do not delay contacting a doctor, a crisis line or emergency services because of anything a mentor has said. If a conversation is heading somewhere that needs a human, please reach one.

Mentor And I is not continuously monitored by a human professional, and messages sent through it are not read in real time.

5. Limits of automated safety systems

Even where automated safety systems exist, they have real limits. They cannot identify every crisis, cannot determine a person's actual level of risk, and can be wrong in both directions — missing genuine danger and reacting to language that was never dangerous.

No safety system we build will change the advice above: when immediate help is needed, contact a human crisis or emergency service.

6. Continuous improvement

Safety rules, model safeguards and evaluation procedures may be updated as the service develops, as applicable law changes and as better safety methods become available.

This page is maintained to match what the product actually does. If you find a discrepancy between what is described here and how Mentor And I behaves, please tell us at support@mentorandi.com.
